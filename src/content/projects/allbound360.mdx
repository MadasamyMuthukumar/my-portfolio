Allbound360 is a LinkedIn growth platform built at GoZen Technologies. It
covers both halves of the funnel — publishing content that attracts inbound
interest, and running outbound prospecting campaigns against it — inside one
multi-tenant product that agencies can operate on behalf of several clients.

I worked across the whole stack: the React interfaces, the NestJS APIs behind
them, the background workers that do the slow work, and the third-party
integrations that connect it all to LinkedIn.

## Inbound — the content engine

The inbound half is built around **Content Brain**, which turns a brief into
publishable LinkedIn content. From there the product handles post creation,
image carousel and video generation, scheduling, and a trackable content
calendar for planning what goes out and when.

The interesting frontend problem here was **generation latency**. Producing a
carousel or a video is not instant, and a spinner for twenty seconds feels
broken. The generation surfaces stream over **SSE**, so text appears as it is
produced and long-running media jobs report progress instead of blocking. The
calendar then had to stay responsive while holding a month of scheduled items,
each with its own live status.

## Outbound — campaigns and sequencing

The outbound half handles lead discovery, campaign sequencing, personalisation,
follow-ups, and the actual LinkedIn execution.

<Figure
  src={outbound}
  alt="The outbound workspace: campaign sequencing and lead management."
  caption="Outbound — campaign sequencing and lead management."
/>

Almost none of this can happen in a request cycle. Campaign steps fire on
schedules, LinkedIn actions have to be paced to stay within platform limits,
and enrichment runs against external sources that fail intermittently. All of
it runs through **BullMQ queues and worker jobs**, with retries and scheduling,
so a slow or failing step is delayed and retried rather than breaking a
campaign.

## AI-SDR — agent-driven qualification

The newest layer applies LLM-driven decisions to the funnel: ICP-driven lead
discovery and qualification, lead enrichment, signal-based workflows, and
scheduled agent runs.

This is where **multi-step agent orchestration** and **tool calling** earn their
place. An agent run reads signals, decides which leads qualify, enriches them,
and hands the result to the same outbound machinery. The engineering problem is
less "call an LLM" and more making non-deterministic decisions safe to run
unattended on a schedule — grounding them in real account data, and keeping
every action reversible and auditable.

## Platform work underneath

- **Multi-tenancy with RBAC** — workspaces and team management shaped for
  agencies, so access is scoped across shared workspaces and members.
- **Integrations** — Auth0 for authentication, Stripe for payments and
  subscriptions, and the LinkedIn APIs for execution.
- **Async everywhere** — queues, Pub/Sub and worker jobs for anything
  long-running or scheduled.
