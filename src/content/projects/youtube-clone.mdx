A UI recreation rather than a working client — the feed reads from local data,
which kept the focus on the interface itself: a Button whose variants are
expressed through `class-variance-authority`, a grid that reflows without
breakpoint-by-breakpoint overrides, and a category bar that measures its own
overflow to decide whether scroll affordances are needed.
